How we work with you

  • Commissioned work. A brief, a budget and a deliverable - the usual shape for an evidence base, a benchmark study or a roadmap.
  • Research partnership. Joining a consortium as a research partner, where the work is genuinely open-ended and the output is knowledge rather than a deliverable to a brief: partner or work package lead in Horizon Europe, LIFE and national programmes, joint publication and open data work.
  • Independent review. A second, disinterested reading of an assessment, a dataset or a claim, written so it can be shown to a board, a regulator or a counterparty.
  • Teaching and facilitation. Training a team, briefing an executive, or running the workshop where a consortium has to agree something.

Work with us

If your work touches any of the above, write to us. We would rather hear the problem than the brief.
