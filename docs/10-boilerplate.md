# Boilerplate

The short texts that carry RISE's positioning everywhere the website does
not: search results, social profiles, proposals and press. From the copy
rewrite of 30 October 2026.

Use these as they are. They are counted against the limits each place
imposes, and rewriting one by eye is how a profile ends up cut off
mid-sentence.

## For proposals, press and event programmes

**One sentence** (26 words):

> RISE is an independent research institute in Vienna that shows what the
> world's building stocks can still emit, and how they can stay within
> planetary limits.

**About RISE, 50 words** (proposals, event programmes):

> RISE is an independent research institute in Vienna. We model building
> stocks across countries to show what fits within the carbon budget, what
> must be avoided, and what it will take for the built environment to give
> back more than it takes. We publish our methods, data and code.

**About RISE, 100 words** (press releases, reports, funder applications):

> RISE (Institute for Regenerative Spatial Systems Science) is an independent
> research institute based in Vienna, with partners and networks across
> Europe and beyond. We work on one question: how much can the world's
> building stocks still take from the planet, and how do we regenerate? We
> combine whole life carbon assessment with building stock modelling to
> produce benchmarks, pathways and open data for the European Commission, UN
> agencies, national governments and funders. Our order of action is avoid,
> reduce, then regenerate. We publish our methods, data and code wherever
> licences allow, and we build models for national teams to run themselves.

## Words to use, and words to retire

From the rewrite. This is the part most likely to drift, because every one of
the retired words is the obvious thing to type.

| Use | Instead of | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| limit, budget, boundary | target, ambition, goal (on their own) | A limit is physical; an ambition is negotiable |
| building stock, pathway, cumulative | building, roadmap, annual | Signals RISE's unit of analysis |
| enough, sufficiency, use what exists | reduce demand, optimise | Concrete and human |
| regenerate, give back (always defined) | net positive, nature-positive, offset | Avoids offsetting language |
| evidence, finding, we found | insight, solution, innovative | Research register, not consultancy |
| open (methods, data, code) | transparent, best-in-class | A claim anyone can check |
| whole life carbon | carbon footprint, green building | Field standard (EN 15978) |
| decarbonisation (as a means) | decarbonisation (as the purpose) | The purpose is staying within limits |
| sustainability (only historically) | sustainable, sustainability | "Beyond sustainability" is the tagline |

Five voice rules, in short: lead with the finding and its source; short
sentences and plain words; say "we"; name the trade-off; and get urgency from
precision rather than from adjectives. "About ten years of 2020 emissions"
does the work that "massive" cannot.
