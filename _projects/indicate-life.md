<h2>Context</h2>

Helps national consortia generate the building LCA data their governments need to set whole life carbon benchmarks and targets.

<h2>Objectives</h2>

- **Fund and support national consortia.** Local teams of researchers,
  industry and government who build their country's building LCA dataset
  themselves, rather than receiving one.
- **Supply the common method.** A shared data model, collection protocol and
  quality criteria, so the results of four countries can be compared without
  any of them being told what to find.
- **Carry it into policy.** The point of the data is benchmarks, target values
  and reporting requirements - so the work runs alongside the people who will
  have to set them.

<h2>Where it runs</h2>

Austria, Croatia, Hungary, Italy and Luxembourg,
under the EU LIFE Clean Energy
Transition programme - following the [pilot](/projects/indicate/) in Ireland,
Spain and Czechia.

<h2>Who is behind it</h2>

INDICATE LIFE is co-ordinated by **Smith Innovation**, with **BPIE**,
**KU Leuven**, **RISE** and the **World Green Building Council** as project
partners providing technical and political support. It is co-funded by the
European Union LIFE programme.
