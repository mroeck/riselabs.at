Targets for the built environment are set globally and measured almost
nowhere. This project builds the tracker that would let progress on whole life
carbon be checked rather than asserted.

<h2>What we are doing</h2>

Our work package covers global building materials and life cycle emissions:

- **Material flows.** How much of what is produced, moved and installed, and
  what that costs in emissions.
- **A repeatable method.** Built so the tracker can be run again next year and
  produce a comparable figure, which is the whole point of a tracker.
- **Honest uncertainty.** Global material data is patchy. Where the numbers are
  weak, the tracker should say so rather than smooth it over.

<h2>Why it matters</h2>

"Whole life carbon is rising" and "whole life carbon is falling" are both
currently defensible claims, because nobody is measuring consistently at this
scale. A tracker settles the question — and makes it possible to tell whether
policy is working.
