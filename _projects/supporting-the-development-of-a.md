<h2>Context</h2>

The European Commission needed a roadmap for reducing whole life carbon of
buildings, and a roadmap needs to know where it is starting from and which
levers actually move the destination.

<h2>Insights</h2>

- **Built the archetype model.** A representation of the EU building stock
  detailed enough to distinguish between policy options, and coarse enough to
  run to 2050.
- **Tested the levers.** Renovation rate, material efficiency, material
  decarbonisation and operational performance, separately and in combination.
- **Set out the trajectory.** What each combination does to the stock's
  emissions over time, and which combinations fall short.

<h2>Impact</h2>

The work fed the Commission's roadmap for reducing whole life carbon of
buildings, and the evidence base behind the whole life carbon provisions now
in the revised Energy Performance of Buildings Directive.
