<h2>Context</h2>

Established a first European baseline for embodied carbon from real building data, and a budget-based way to set targets against it.

<h2>Insights</h2>

- **Assembled real building data.** A European dataset of embodied carbon from
  assessed buildings, rather than from modelled archetypes.
- **Derived a baseline.** What the industry currently builds, expressed so that
  a given project can tell whether it is better or worse than normal.
- **Proposed a budget-based route to targets.** Deriving limit values from a
  carbon budget rather than from current practice, so the target reflects what
  the climate requires and not what the market already does.

<h2>Why it matters</h2>

A benchmark taken from current practice makes the average acceptable by
definition. A budget-based target does not, which is why the distinction keeps
recurring in the policy work that followed.
