A government cannot set a whole life carbon benchmark without data on its own
building stock, and most governments do not have it. The usual answer is to
commission a study, which produces one number and no capability.

INDICATE piloted a different answer: fund and support a national consortium to
build the data capability itself.

<h2>What we did</h2>

- **Piloted in three countries.** Ireland, Spain and Czechia, each with its own
  consortium of local researchers, industry and government.
- **Supplied the method, not the answer.** Common data model, collection
  protocol and quality criteria, so results would be comparable between
  countries without being dictated.
- **Tested transferability.** Whether the approach depends on a particular
  market structure, or works wherever there is a willing consortium.

<h2>What came of it</h2>

It transferred. The pilot became [INDICATE
LIFE](/projects/indicate-life/), which now runs in Austria,
Hungary, Italy and Luxembourg under the EU LIFE programme.
