Our paper on context-specific life cycle emissions pathways for EU buildings
and construction is out in *Nature Communications*, with colleagues at
KU Leuven, TU Graz and IIASA.

The question behind it: decarbonisation scenarios for the European building
stock are usually run at aggregate level, which hides how differently they play
out across countries, building types and construction practices. We model those
differences explicitly, and the pathways that result are not interchangeable.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73433-1)
