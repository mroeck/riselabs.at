The Global Building Data Initiative hosted **"Advancing Whole Life Carbon
(WLC) & Emissions Accounting for India's Building & Construction Sector"** on
**1 July 2026**, co-organised with the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay,
RISE, the Alliance for an Energy Efficient Economy (AEEE) and Greentech
Knowledge Solutions.

<h2>What was covered</h2>

- Why whole life carbon matters for India's net-zero goals
- What India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS) and the EU's Carbon Border
  Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) mean for material supply chains
- The data, methods and tools available for tracking life cycle emissions in
  Indian construction

Speakers included Dr Zuhaib Batra (Senior Scientist and Global Policy Lead,
GBDI / EPFL), Dr Martin Röck (Executive Director and Lead Scientist, GBDI /
RISE), Prof. Albert Thomas (IIT Bombay), Saswati Chetia (Greentech Knowledge
Solutions) and Dr Brijesh Pandey (AEEE).

This webinar is part of the
[GBDI China and India Cooperation Incubator](/projects/gbdi-china-and-india-cooperation/),
which extends the global building data work into the two markets that will add
the most floor area this century.
