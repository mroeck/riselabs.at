The 24th **CII–IGBC Green Building Congress** runs from **26 to 28 November
2026** at the Jio World Convention Centre in Mumbai, under the theme
*Forging a Sustainable Future — Do Good, Feel Good. Building Greener Worlds
Together*.

Martin Röck has been invited to join the opening **Leadership Panel, "Global
Pathways to Green, Resilient & Net-Zero Buildings and Built Environments"**,
on **26 November**, immediately following the inaugural session. The panel
brings together international perspectives alongside the National Institute of
Urban Affairs and senior leaders from across the built environment sector.

The discussion covers decarbonisation, building performance and data,
regenerative systems, climate resilience, circularity, policy and innovation —
and how international evidence translates into something usable in a market
building at India's pace.

<h2>Why this one matters to us</h2>

India is among the two largest green building markets in the world by
registered footprint, and most of the floor area of the coming decades will be
built in countries where the data infrastructure for whole life carbon does
not yet exist. That is the gap the
[Global Building Data Initiative](/projects/global-building-data-initiative/)
was set up to close, and it is why we keep showing up in these rooms.

The Congress is convened by the Indian Green Building Council, part of the
Confederation of Indian Industry, with participation from the World Green
Building Council and the Government of Maharashtra as partner state.
