**Help show which buildings and materials fit within the carbon budget**, so
that construction can meet human needs within planetary limits. You will lead
our scientific work on whole life cycle assessment of buildings, materials and
building stocks: the material basis of human settlements. You will develop the
methods and data that make WLCA comparable and usable worldwide, for GBDI and
INDICATE, build GBDI's data partnerships in Asia and Africa, and help shape
GBDI's open building data format (openBDF).

You will work directly with the Director and the Global Partnerships and
Programmes Lead (RISE-Team-2611-01), and closely with our Junior Scientist for
whole life carbon and LCA (RISE-Team-2611-03) and GBDI's software engineers.

This profile is tentative. We will design the final profile and the extent
together with the person we hire, based on their strengths, interests and our
project needs. We value initiative: if you see a better way to contribute,
tell us.

<h2>What you will do</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- Develop regional material emission factors and prospective WLCA methods, and
  specify checks against benchmarks and limit values for GBDI's open-source
  WLCA calculation engine.
- Identify and onboard data partners in Asia and Africa, and co-develop
  emission factors, openBDF adaptations and trainings with local universities
  and partners.
- Contribute to the global WLCA data standard developed in CLEAR on the basis
  of openBDF.
- Lead INDICATE's technical onboarding pathway for new countries: minimum data
  requirements, quality assurance, open-source tools and templates, aligned
  with the EPBD, EPC databases and national building registries. Co-lead
  guidance on linking whole life carbon with EPCs, Renovation and Material
  Passports, CRREM and GRESB.
- Upscale results to building stocks and scenarios, including
  renovation-first, circular and low-carbon material strategies. Publish,
  supervise and help raise funding.

</div>

<h2>What you bring</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- **Essential.** Experience with scaling up building LCA, material flow
  analysis, building stock modelling or scenario analysis, ideally including
  carbon budgets and sufficiency-first strategies (avoid, shift, improve).
- **Essential.** A publication record in peer-reviewed journals and experience
  leading or contributing to research proposals or grants. Tell us which funds
  you helped raise and what your role was.
- A PhD, or a Master's degree with at least 3 years of relevant research
  experience, in industrial ecology, environmental or civil engineering,
  architecture or a closely related field.
- Proven expertise in LCA of buildings and construction products according to
  EN 15978, EN 15804+A2 and ISO 14040/44, including whole life carbon, life
  cycle inventory and emission factor data.
- Demonstrated coding experience in Python and reproducible workflows (Git,
  documented code). Name the frameworks and packages you have used, for
  example brightway, premise, openLCA, ODYM, pandas or polars.
- Professional proficiency in English, spoken and written. German is
  appreciated but not required.

</div>

<h2>Nice to have</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- Experience with data standards, schemas or exchange formats (JSON schema,
  APIs), EPDs or LCA databases.
- Experience with input-output or parametric modelling of building stocks, or
  with impacts beyond global warming potential, such as land use, biodiversity
  or life cycle costs.
- Knowledge of EU whole life carbon policy (EPBD, Level(s)) and of building
  practices and data in our focus countries.
- Experience supervising students or junior researchers.

</div>
