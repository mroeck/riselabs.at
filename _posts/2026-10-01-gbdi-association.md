The Global Building Data Initiative has been established as a non-profit association
under Austrian law, led by RISE director Dr. Martin Röck and core collaborator Dr. Karen Scrivener of EPFL. Until now GBDI has run as a joint programme inside a research consortium with RISE and EPFL; as an association it gains a legal home,a governance structure, and the ability to hold research funding and employ staff directly.

For RISE this changes little day to day and quite a lot structurally. The two teams will continue to work alongside each other in Vienna, and some colleagues will hold contracts with both. But GBDI can now grow on its own terms, as an
open data commons NPO available to a wider community rather than any single
research project.

More on the initiative at [gbdi.io](https://www.gbdi.io).
