INDICATE LIFE closes with a full day at **L42, Rue de la Loi 42, Brussels, on
Wednesday 21 October 2026**, bringing together policymakers, industry and
stakeholders from across Europe.

<h2>Morning: the WLC Network, 09:30–13:00</h2>

Roundtable discussions, introduced by the roundtable leads and Smith
Innovation, on four questions the sector has not yet answered:

- How should Member States turn building-level data into credible benchmarks,
  targets and regulatory limit values?
- How should whole life carbon thinking influence renovation policy, and
  decisions about renovation, reuse or rebuild?
- How can whole life carbon data better inform investment, lending, valuation
  and development decisions?
- What data infrastructure, skills, responsibilities and market conditions are
  needed for implementation at scale?

Key points from the roundtables are presented at 11:30, followed by lunch and
networking.

<h2>Afternoon: the closing event, 14:00–17:00</h2>

Opened by Giulia Carbonari (World Green Building Council), with an interview
with Ciarán Cuffe moderated by Miles Rowland (WorldGBC). Zsolt Toth (BPIE)
moderates the panel discussion; Martin Röck joins the panel alongside Paul
Astle (Ramboll), with DG ENER and EBC to be confirmed. Josefina Lindblom
(DG ENV) presents the Staff Working Document, and Adam Rietti (Smith
Innovation) sets out what INDICATE LIFE achieved. Networking and a cocktail
close the day.
