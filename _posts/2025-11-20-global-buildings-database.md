We have published the global buildings database openly: whole life cycle
greenhouse gas emissions, energy performance and material intensity, assembled
with partners across the GBDI network.

It is the evidence base the initiative was built to create, and it is free for
anyone to use, check and extend.

[Get the data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8378938)
