**Turn evidence into decisions.** You will lead the partnerships and global
programmes that move whole life carbon from research into policy and markets
worldwide. You will grow our cooperation in Asia (India, China) and Africa
(South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Ethiopia), connect it to EU policy through the
INDICATE network, and support GBDI's work in CLEAR with RICS and WBCSD. You
will turn contacts into funded projects and help build the organisations that
carry this work beyond 2028, working directly with the Director and our
scientists.

This profile is tentative. We will design the final profile and the extent
together with the person we hire, based on their strengths, interests and our
project needs. We value initiative: if you see a better way to contribute,
tell us.

<h2>What you will do</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- Coordinate GBDI's cooperation in India and China, including joint projects
  with CABR, Tsinghua University, CBMF and UNEP GlobalABC: co-creation
  workshops, webinars and a joint white paper.
- Build fair, long-term partnerships in Africa (Kenya, Ethiopia, Nigeria,
  South Africa) with our Senior Scientist for whole life carbon and LCA
  (RISE-Team-2611-02), in which local partners own their data and results.
- Support EPBD implementation and strategic engagement in European policy,
  finance and industry fora, and contribute to INDICATE's business model,
  governance and fundraising.
- Support GBDI's role in CLEAR with RICS and WBCSD: coordinate our
  contributions to the harmonised WLCA framework, the policy playbook and the
  training packages.
- Help build up the new GBDI non-profit association, manage partner
  commitments and project plans, and track what our work changes, such as
  requirements adopted, datasets in use and funding secured beyond 2028.
- Identify and pursue philanthropic, multilateral, EU and private funding, and
  lead proposals from concept to budget and submission.

</div>

<h2>What you bring</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- Essential: experience building partnerships and coalitions with government, industry, research and philanthropy, ideally across countries and regions.
- Essential: strong writing and presentation skills, and confidence in representing an organisation at high-level policy and industry fora.
- A Master's degree with at least 3 years of relevant professional experience,
  or a PhD, in the built environment, sustainability, climate policy,
  international development or a related field.
- A proven fundraising track record, for example successful proposals or
  grants. Tell us which funds you helped raise and what your role was.
- A good understanding of whole life carbon, life cycle assessment of
  buildings and the policy landscape for decarbonising buildings and
  construction, including carbon budgets and sufficiency-first approaches.
- Professional proficiency in English, spoken and written. German is
  appreciated but not required.

</div>

<h2>Nice to have</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- A network in, or work experience with, one or more of our focus countries
  (India, China, South Africa, Kenya, Nigeria, Ethiopia), and a language
  spoken there.
- Familiarity with initiatives such as UNEP GlobalABC, the Buildings
  Breakthrough or CLEAR, and with international frameworks for whole life
  carbon reporting.
- Experience with impact monitoring, evaluation and learning for research and
  policy programmes.
- Openness to occasional travel for partner meetings and events.

</div>
