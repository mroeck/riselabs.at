**Help build the evidence that shows which buildings and materials fit within
the carbon budget.** You will develop the data and models behind our work on
whole life carbon of buildings, materials and building stocks: the material
basis of human settlements.

You will work with building-level LCA data and national building data, such as
EPC databases, building registries and generic LCA databases, to define what
data countries need, develop benchmarks, and build open tools and templates
for INDICATE and GBDI with partners in Europe, Asia and Africa. You will work
closely with our Senior Scientist for whole life carbon and LCA
(RISE-Team-2611-02). The position can be combined with a PhD.

This profile is tentative. We will design the final profile and the extent
together with the person we hire, based on their strengths, interests and our
project needs. We value initiative: if you see a better way to contribute,
tell us.

<h2>What you will do</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- Collect, harmonise and analyse building-level WLCA data and national
  building data (EPC databases, building registries), including conversion to
  openBDF.
- Support the definition of minimum data requirements and data quality
  criteria, and develop open-source templates and tools for INDICATE's
  onboarding pathway.
- Develop benchmarks and analyse whole life carbon of buildings and building
  stocks, including upscaling to stock level and scenarios.
- Support analyses that link whole life carbon with EPCs, Renovation Passports
  and finance frameworks such as CRREM and GRESB.
- Prepare figures, datasets and documentation for publications, reports and
  trainings.

</div>

<h2>What you bring</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- **Essential.** Knowledge of life cycle assessment and material flow
  analysis, ideally applied to buildings, and an interest in carbon budgets
  and sufficiency-first strategies.
- **Essential.** Demonstrated coding experience in Python (pandas, numpy and
  the like) and in working with large datasets. Name the tools and packages
  you have worked with.
- A Master's degree in industrial ecology, environmental or civil engineering,
  architecture, geoinformatics, data science or a related field.
- An independent, careful and structured way of working, and the wish to grow
  as a researcher.
- Professional proficiency in English, spoken and written. German is
  appreciated but not required.

</div>

<h2>Nice to have</h2>

<div class="findings" markdown="1">

- Experience with GIS or remote sensing for stock characterisation, such as
  QGIS, geopandas or Google Earth Engine.
- Experience with EPC or building registry data, LCA databases or EPDs.
- A language spoken in one of our focus countries: India, China, South Africa,
  Kenya, Nigeria, Ethiopia.
- Plans to pursue a PhD in this field. We co-supervise doctoral projects with
  partner universities.

</div>
