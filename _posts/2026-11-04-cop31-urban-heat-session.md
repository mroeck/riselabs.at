<h2>What we bring to it</h2>

Decarbonisation scenarios for the European building stock are usually run at
aggregate level, which hides how differently they play out across countries,
building types and construction practices. The
[paper behind this talk](/publications/alaux-2026-context-specific-life/),
published in *Nature Communications* with KU Leuven, TU Graz and IIASA,
models those differences explicitly, and the pathways that result are not
interchangeable.

That is the point worth carrying into a session about cooling and clean air:
what works in one country's stock is not a template for another's, and a
pathway that averages them is a pathway nobody can follow.
