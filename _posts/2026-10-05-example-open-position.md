We are looking for a researcher to join RISE in Vienna, working on whole life
carbon scenarios for European and global building stocks.

**What the role involves.** Building and running bottom-up building stock
models; analysing material and energy flows and the emissions that follow from
them; turning that into evidence policy makers can act on. You would work
across our European projects and with the GBDI team next door.

**What we are looking for.** A background in civil or environmental
engineering, architecture, industrial ecology or a related field; real
competence with data analysis in Python; and an interest in what the numbers
are for.

**How to apply.** Write to [office@riselabs.at](mailto:office@riselabs.at)
with a CV and a short note on what draws you to the work. The terms and the
closing date are in the box at the top of this page.
