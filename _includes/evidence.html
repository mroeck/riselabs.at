{%- comment -%} The two evidence lines under an Expertise field: the projects where the work was done, and the publications where it can be checked. include.projects slugs in _projects/ include.publications slugs in _publications/ include.posts slugs in _posts/, without the date Publications and posts share the second line, publications first, because to a reader they are the same thing: somewhere to go and read it. Nothing here carries a title. Every one is pulled in by slug, so editing a publication's title changes it everywhere at once and nothing on this page can drift. docs-check.py fails a slug with no file behind it. Titles over 80 characters are cut at a word boundary, because several run past 180 and would push the line to four rows on a phone. The full title stays in the link's `title`, so hovering or a screen reader gets all of it. Liquid has no filter that does this: `truncate` cuts mid-word and `truncatewords` counts words, not characters, so the cut is made by truncating one character long and dropping whatever partial word is left. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Used directly rather than through `default:`: Liquid has no literal for an empty array, and `nil.size` is already 0, so the guards below work as they are on a field that passes none. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign projects = include.projects -%} {%- assign publications = include.publications -%} {%- assign posts = include.posts -%} {%- if projects.size > 0 %}
