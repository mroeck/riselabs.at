{%- comment -%} A list of publications, newest first. Expects: include.pubs include.sections="true" breaks them into kind sections. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Order: newest first, and within one year the team's own RISE or GBDI work ahead of their earlier work. Someone scanning a year wants to see what RISE did that year before what its people did elsewhere before joining. Liquid cannot sort on two keys, so the list is sorted by year and then rebuilt a year at a time, RISE work first. `rise_work` is a field on each publication rather than something worked out here: a template cannot sort on a value it computes while rendering, and docs-check.py keeps the field honest against the `rise_since` years it is derived from. `pubs` starts as an empty array of the right kind, produced by a `where` that matches nothing. Jekyll's Liquid has no literal for one. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign by_year = include.pubs | sort: "year" | reverse -%} {%- assign years = by_year | map: "year" | uniq -%} {%- assign pubs = include.pubs | where: "__never__", "__never__" -%} {%- for y in years -%} {%- assign in_year = by_year | where: "year", y -%} {%- assign ours = in_year | where: "rise_work", true -%} {%- assign theirs = in_year | where_exp: "x", "x.rise_work != true" -%} {%- assign pubs = pubs | concat: ours | concat: theirs -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- if include.sections == "true" -%} {%- assign kinds = "article,book,report,data" | split: "," -%} {%- assign names = "Journal articles,Books and chapters,Reports,Data and tools" | split: "," -%} {%- for k in kinds -%} {%- assign group = pubs | where: "kind", k -%} {%- if group.size > 0 %}

{{ names[forloop.index0] }}

    {%- for pub in group %}{% include pubitem.html pub=pub %}{% endfor -%}
{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- else -%}
    {%- for pub in pubs %}{% include pubitem.html pub=pub %}{% endfor -%}
{%- endif -%}
