{%- comment -%} People as cards. Expects: include.people Each card shows a square portrait, the name, the role, the employer line, and a row with the person's email and LinkedIn. No tagline here. A tagline is one or two sentences, so a card carrying one is two or three times the height of a card without, and a grid of cards where one is three times its neighbour reads as broken rather than as emphasis. The tagline belongs on the person's own page, where there is room for it to be read rather than skimmed. Only email and LinkedIn on the card, even where the person's front matter carries more. Every card then has the same two rows, so the cards are the same shape; the rest of a person's links - a profile at their university, an ORCID, a personal site - are on their own page, which is where someone who wants them is heading anyway. Where there is no portrait yet, the card shows the person's initials on a token-coloured square rather than a stock photograph. A stock face standing in for a real person is forbidden by docs/06, and an empty box reads as a fault. The initials come from `cite` ("Röck M" gives RM), which everyone already carries because the publication lists need it; `name` would give "DM" for "Dr. Martin Röck". The affiliation line is the point of the single grid: the person's own institutions for our own people, "Extended team · " for those employed elsewhere. See docs/06 for why that distinction is kept visible. "Extended team", not "core collaborator" (R8, decided 29 Oct 2026): the older phrase named people by what they are not, which is a poor way to introduce a colleague. The front-matter key is still `group: collaborator`, because it is an internal key and renaming it would touch every file for no gain. Portrait alt text is deliberately empty here, because the name is printed next to it. The profile page carries the name as alt instead. {%- endcomment -%}
