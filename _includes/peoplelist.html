{%- comment -%} People as cards. Expects: include.people Each card shows a square portrait, the name, the role, the employer line, the tagline, and a row of links. Where there is no portrait yet, the card shows the person's initials on a token-coloured square rather than a stock photograph. A stock face standing in for a real person is forbidden by docs/06, and an empty box reads as a fault. The initials come from `cite` ("Röck M" gives RM), which everyone already carries because the publication lists need it; `name` would give "DM" for "Dr. Martin Röck". The employer line is the point of the single grid: "RISE" for our own people, "Core collaborator · " for those employed elsewhere. See docs/06 for why that distinction is kept visible. Portrait alt text is deliberately empty here, because the name is printed next to it. The profile page carries the name as alt instead. {%- endcomment -%}
