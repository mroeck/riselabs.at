Expects: include.pub Any RISE person's `cite` name is underlined automatically. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign p = include.pub -%} {%- assign a = p.authors -%} {%- comment -%} Every form a person has published under, not just the current one. A name in an author string is the name that paper carries, and people's names change: Zuhaib published as "Zuhaib S" until 2022 and as "Batra Z" from 2024, under one ORCID. Matching only the current form would leave half of someone's own work looking like a stranger's. `cite` first, then anything in `cite_also`. The longest form has to go first within a person, or a shorter one can match inside a longer one and break the markup. {%- endcomment -%} {%- for person in site.people -%} {%- if person.cite -%} {%- capture wrapped %}{{ person.cite }}{% endcapture -%} {%- assign a = a | replace: person.cite, wrapped -%} {%- endif -%} {%- for also in person.cite_also -%} {%- capture wrapped_also %}{{ also }}{% endcapture -%} {%- assign a = a | replace: also, wrapped_also -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%}
