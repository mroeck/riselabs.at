{%- comment -%} "Coming up": the events still ahead, soonest first, at most three (N3). Used on /news/ and, through P3.2, on the home page. An event counts as ahead when its `event_end` (or `event_start`, or the first of its `event_month`) is today or later. Dates are compared as YYYYMMDD integers, which sorts and compares correctly and avoids Liquid's date arithmetic entirely. `event_month` is for a date known only to the month, which is the COP31 session: the conference dates are published, the session's day is not (H8). It shows as the month and year with a line saying the date is to be confirmed, and sorts on the first of the month. Staleness: GitHub Pages rebuilds only on push, so a build-time split goes out of date the moment nobody pushes for a week. Every item carries `data-end`, and ui.js hides the ones whose end has passed.
