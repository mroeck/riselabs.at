## Abstract

The introduction of the Directive 2010/31/EU has stimulated innovative
advances in the domain of nearly-zero energy building retrofits and several
performance-based solutions relating to the retrofit of building façades.
Façade is a critical area of intervention in deep building retrofits. Façade
designers, engineers and/or architects must emphasise on and render customised
retrofit solutions for varied building types and focus on more than just
achieving low-energy consumption buildings. Façades are the prime face of
interaction and have deep visual and functional impact on the built
environment. It is essential to investigate and review the current trends and
focus parameters of façade retrofits by examining the state-of-art research on
retrofits. The goal of this study is to establish a brief repository for the
building professionals, to inform the current state-of-art and modernisation
trends. Thus, it aims at developing fundamental aspects of façade retrofit
practices towards achieving better quality nearly zero-energy buildings.
