## Abstract

This dataset provides per EU country 1) a detailed life cycle inventory (LCI),
and 2) a life cycle impact assessment (LCIA) of both existing and newly
constructed building archetypes representing the EU27 building stock. For each
archetype, the LCI records annual material and energy flows, broken down by
life cycle stage (according to EN 15978), and building element (such as
external walls, pitched roofs or heating system). The dataset specifies the
type of activity (e.g., material entering or leaving the system, energy use)
and quantifies the associated material or energy amounts. The LCIA provides
per building archetype the environmental impacts for 19 environmental impact
indicators (in line with EN 15804+A2:2019) aggregated per element class,
activity year and life cycle stage. This structure enables a comprehensive
analysis of embodied and operational impacts, supporting robust scenario
modelling, policy assessment, and benchmarking for decarbonization strategies
in the built environment.
