## Abstract

A public repository on Embodied & Whole Life Carbon Data of Buildings across
Europe. The repository provides the building LCA data compiled, processed and
analyzed as well as the scripts used for processing, analysis and
visualisation. Developed by Martin Röck (KU Leuven) and Andreas Sorensen
(Ramboll) within the 'Towards embodied carbon benchmarks for buildings in
Europe' project.
